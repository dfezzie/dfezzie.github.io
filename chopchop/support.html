Common Questions

How do I invite someone to my household?
Open the Account tab, tap your household, and tap "Create invite code." Share the 6-letter code with the person you want to add. They can enter it from the Account tab on their device.

How do I leave a household or switch to a new one?
Only the household owner can remove members today. Email dfezzie@gmail.com if you need help leaving a household or transferring ownership.

How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the email link. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or email dfezzie@gmail.com.

How do I delete my account?
Email dfezzie@gmail.com from the address on your account and your account plus all associated data will be removed within a reasonable time frame.
